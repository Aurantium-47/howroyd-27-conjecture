# Changelog

## v0.2.0 — formal proof and public documentation

- Added the Lean 4 proof of the 27-set theorem for every d >= 10 with 3 not dividing d, with source, build instructions, and verification records.
- The proof has compiled locally and reports only the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The divisible-by-three branch is not proved in this project; no external independent kernel check or human expert review has been completed.
- Simplified public descriptions of AI use and manuscript preparation.
- Retained mathematical attribution, reproducibility instructions, and the scope of completed verification.
- The mathematical conclusions, certificate, and existing Python verification algorithms are unchanged.

## v0.1.0 — initial public research draft

- Manuscript proving and classifying the 27 maximum independent sets for all d >= 10 with 3 not dividing d.
- Complete 89-entry exact certificate, original verifier, separately written finite checks, and local run results.
- Explicit attribution of the known divisible-by-three branch and existing covering methods.
- AI use, review status, and reproducibility documentation.
